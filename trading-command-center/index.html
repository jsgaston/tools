Deposits do not distort the return

Account reports show the return on starting capital and a time-weighted return that chains each stretch between deposits and withdrawals, so topping up the account does not look like trading profit.

Each bot keeps its own trades

A position belongs to the magic number that opened it. If you close it by hand, or another EA closes it, the result still counts for the bot that took the trade. That is what makes the per-bot reports and the ranking honest.

Reports describe what already happened on your account. They do not forecast anything.

Built so a phone cannot wreck an account

Remote control is useful right up to the moment someone else has your phone, or you mistype a lot size. These guards are always there.

Off until you say so

Opening and closing from the chat is disabled by default. Alerts and reports work without it.

Only your chat

Commands from any other chat or group are ignored. Your bot token stays in your own terminal.

Confirm, or nothing happens

Orders and close-all show what will be done and wait for a tap. After two minutes the request expires.

Kill-switch that sticks

/stop blocks new trades and survives a restart of the EA or the terminal. Closing and stop changes remain available.

Risk guard

Maximum lot size, open positions, daily loss, risk per trade when a stop is set, and spread. An order outside them is refused with the reason.

Symbol whitelist

Limit which symbols may be opened from the chat, so a typo cannot land on an instrument you never trade.

What it does not do

It does not decide trades or give signals. It reports and executes what you, or your own EAs, decide. Leverage can cost more than a deposit, and a remote order is still a real order.

Questions

Is there a free trial?

No. MetaTrader 5 blocks internet requests inside the Strategy Tester, so a tool that talks to Telegram cannot be shown there, and there is no demo licence. The monthly plan is the way to try it: one month, cancel from your subscription page, and it stops at the end of the paid period. This page and the command list show exactly what you get.

Does it work with EAs from other developers?

Yes. It reads the account, not a particular EA, so it reports trades from any Expert Advisor, from your phone app or from the web terminal. Give each EA its own magic number and the per-bot reports separate them.

Can it open trades on my account without me?

No. It never decides a trade. Orders from the chat are disabled by default, need your confirmation when enabled, respect the risk guard, and only your own chat is obeyed.

What happens if my internet or the licence server goes down?

The licence is checked when the EA starts and every few hours. If the check server cannot be reached, a recent valid check keeps it working for up to three days. Telegram alerts need your terminal to reach Telegram, as they always would.

Do I need a VPS?

Only if you want alerts while your computer is off. It runs wherever MT5 runs and stays open: a PC left on, or a Windows VPS near your broker.

What data does it send to Wisfli?

Only what the licence check needs: your key, your MT5 account number and server, and whether the account is demo or live. Trades, balances, reports and your Telegram token stay between your terminal and your chat. Details are in the privacy policy.

I changed broker. Do I need a new key?

No. The same key works on up to three live and three demo accounts. When you need a slot, free an old account on your licence page with your key and purchase email.

How do I cancel the subscription?

From the subscription link in your licence email, with the email you paid with. The EA keeps working until the end of the month you already paid and then stops on its own. Nothing else to do.
