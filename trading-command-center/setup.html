Setup guide

From the email to your first alert

About ten minutes, and most of it is creating your Telegram bot. Nothing here needs programming.

0What you need

  • MetaTrader 5 installed on a Windows PC or a VPS, and left open. If the terminal is closed, nothing is sent.
  • Your licence email from licenses@wisfli.com, with the key and the .ex5 file attached.
  • A Telegram account, or a Discord server where you can create a webhook. You can use both.

One copy on one chart watches the whole account. You do not need one per symbol or per EA.

1Install the file

  1. Save TradingCommandCenter.ex5 from your email to your computer. If your email provider strips the attachment, use the download button in the same email.
  2. In MetaTrader 5: File > Open Data Folder.
  3. Open MQL5, then Experts, and copy the file there.
  4. Back in MT5, right-click anywhere in the Navigator panel (Ctrl+N) and choose Refresh. Trading Command Center now appears under Expert Advisors.

2Allow three web addresses

MetaTrader blocks internet access unless you list the addresses yourself.

  1. Tools > Options > Expert Advisors.
  2. Tick Allow WebRequest for listed URL.
  3. Add these three, one per line:
    • https://api.telegram.org
    • https://discord.com
    • https://api.wisfli.com
  4. OK.

api.wisfli.com is needed even if you only use Discord: it is where the licence is checked. Do not add discordapp.com: it is the old address and MetaTrader does not follow its redirect.

3Create your Telegram bot

The bot is yours. Its token stays in your terminal and is never sent to Wisfli.

  1. In Telegram, search for @BotFather (the one with the blue check) and open the chat.
  2. Send /newbot.
  3. Give it a display name, for example My MT5 alerts.
  4. Give it a username ending in bot, for example javier_mt5_alerts_bot. If it is taken, try another.
  5. BotFather replies with a token that looks like 1234567890:AAF.... Copy it and keep it private: anyone with it can use your bot.
  6. Open a chat with your new bot and send it any message, for example "hello". Telegram will not let a bot write to you first.

4Find your chat ID

The chat ID says where the messages go. Pick one of the two ways.

Easy way

In Telegram, search for @userinfobot, open it and press start. It replies with your Id: a number like 123456789. That is your chat ID for a private chat with your bot.

Way that also works for groups

  1. If you want the alerts in a group, create it, add your bot as a member and write any message there.
  2. In your browser open this address, replacing TOKEN with your bot token:
    https://api.telegram.org/botTOKEN/getUpdates
  3. Look for "chat":{"id": in the text. That number is your chat ID. For groups it starts with a minus sign, for example -1001234567890, and the minus is part of the ID.

5Discord (optional)

  1. In your Discord server, open the channel settings (the cog next to the channel name).
  2. Integrations > Webhooks > New Webhook.
  3. Give it a name and press Copy Webhook URL.
  4. It must start with https://discord.com/api/webhooks/. If it starts with discordapp.com, change that part by hand.

Discord receives alerts and reports. Buttons and commands are Telegram only, because Discord webhooks cannot send them.

6Start it and activate your licence

  1. Open any chart on the account you want to watch. The symbol does not matter, though the on-demand screenshot will be of that chart.
  2. Check that the Algo Trading button in the toolbar is green.
  3. Drag TradingCommandCenter from the Navigator onto the chart.
  4. In the Common tab, tick Allow Algo Trading.
  5. In Inputs, fill in:
    • InpLicenseKey: your key, WSF-XXXXX-XXXXX-XXXXX-XXXXX
    • InpTelegramToken and InpTelegramChatID, and/or InpDiscordWebhook
  6. OK.

Within seconds you get a start message with your balance and the button menu. The account is now linked to your licence automatically.

Without a valid key the EA stays on the chart, writes the reason in the top left corner and sends nothing. Without any channel configured it removes itself, because there would be nowhere to write.

7Daily use

Alerts arrive on their own. For everything else, use the buttons or type a command. English and Spanish forms both work.

To separate your EAs in the reports, give each one its own magic number and list them in InpReportMagics. They then appear as buttons under 🤖 Bots.

8Trading from the chat

Disabled by default. Turn it on by setting InpEnableTrading to true, and only if you understand that anyone with access to your Telegram account could then send orders.

CommandWhat it does
/buy EURUSD 0.10Market buy. Optional: /buy EURUSD 0.10 SL TP, as prices
/sell XAUUSD 0.05 3380 3320Market sell with stop loss and take profit
/sltp TICKET SL TPChange both levels. 0 removes one, = keeps it
/sl TICKET PRICE · /tp TICKET PRICEChange one level
/close TICKET · /closeallClose one position, or all of them

Orders and close-all always ask for confirmation, and the request expires after two minutes. Keep InpRestrictToChatID on true so only your chat is obeyed, and set InpMaxLot to something you would never exceed by accident.

10Accounts, updates and cancelling

Your key works on 3 live and 3 demo accounts at the same time. Accounts link themselves the first time the EA runs on them.

  • Change accounts: go to your licence page, enter your key and the email you bought with, and free the account you no longer use. Up to three changes every 30 days. Demo accounts unused for 30 days are freed automatically.
  • Updates: download the newest file from that same page and replace the old one in MQL5\Experts. Your key and settings stay the same.
  • Monthly plan: cancel from the subscription link in your licence email, or from the link on the product page. It keeps working until the end of the month you already paid.
  • Lost your key? Write to support@wisfli.com from the email you bought with and a new key is issued.

11If something does not work

Look at the Experts tab at the bottom of MetaTrader: the EA writes the reason there in plain English.

What you seeWhat to do
"Enter your license key in the EA inputs"The key field is empty. Paste the key from your email
"Invalid license key"Check for a missing character. The key has the form WSF-XXXXX-XXXXX-XXXXX-XXXXX
"Account limit reached"You are using more accounts than your licence allows. Free one on your licence page
"Cannot verify the license (no connection)"The terminal cannot reach api.wisfli.com. Check the URL list, your firewall or your VPS connection. A previous valid check keeps it working for up to 72 hours
"Allow WebRequest for https://api.wisfli.com"Step 2 of this guide is missing or misspelled
Error 4060, or nothing arrives in Telegramapi.telegram.org is not in the allowed list, or the token or chat ID is wrong. Remember to write to your bot first
The EA removes itself from the chartNo channel configured. Fill in the Telegram token and chat ID, or the Discord webhook
Telegram works, Discord is silentCheck the webhook URL starts with discord.com, and that discord.com is in the allowed list. Discord gets alerts and reports, not menus
No screenshotsImages are attached only when the trade is on the same symbol as the chart. /photo always sends one
The licence email never arrivedCheck spam. If it is not there, write to support@wisfli.com with the email you paid with
